## 🎓 Academic Context

Most of these projects were developed as part of my curriculum for BS in Applied AI and Data Science at IIT Jodhpur, specifically focusing on the subject **"Visualization and Storytelling with Data."**

## 📊 Featured Projects

  1. Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end business intelligence solution developed to analyze retail performance across the United States.

   - **Objective**: To identify profitability trends and regional sales performance.

   - **Dataset**: Sample - Superstore (XLS).

   - **Key Techniques**: Data Modeling (Star Schema), DAX Measures, ETL (Power Query), Map Visualizations.

   - **Insights Uncovered**: Identified that while Technology drives high revenue, specific sub-categories in Furniture (like Tables) are significantly impacting overall profit margins due to high discount rates.


## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills

   - **Tools**: Power BI Desktop, Power Query, Excel (XLS/CSV).

   - **Data Modeling**: Relationships (One-to-Many), Schema Design.

   - **Analytics**: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculated columns and measures.

   - **Storytelling**: Narrative-driven layout design, color theory for data, and audience-centric reporting.
