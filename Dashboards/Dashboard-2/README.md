**Subject:** Visualization and Storytelling with Data

**Institution:** Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur

## 🎯 The Mission

 To transform a complex retail dataset into a high-level executive narrative. This dashboard identifies why certain targets are being missed and where the next growth opportunity lies by bridging the gap between raw data and business strategy.

## 🛠️ Data Engineering & Modeling

**Before the visuals, I processed and modeled the backend to ensure a "Single Source of Truth":**

 * Scalability: Processed 9,900+ rows of the 'Orders' dataset.

 * Relational Mapping: Established complex relationships between Orders, Returns, and Regional Managers (People) tables.

 * Geospatial Processing: Handled geographical data categorization to enable accurate mapping across US regions.

 * DAX & ETL: Used Power Query for noise reduction and DAX for dynamic target-vs-actual calculations.

## 🚀 Key Story Points & Insights

 - The Growth Gap: Visualizing the delta between $2.30M (Actual) vs $3.68M (Target) sales using dynamic gauge charts.

 - The "Tables" Paradox: A critical insight revealed that while Technology drives the highest revenue ($836K), certain Furniture sub-categories (like Tables) show negative margins despite high sales volume, signaling a need for immediate pricing strategy revisions.

 - Regional Dominance: A donut analysis proving that the West Region ($108K profit) is the primary driver of the company's bottom line.

 - Efficiency Champion: Office Supplies maintains stable margins across high quantity, serving as the business's operational backbone.

## 📈 Visualization Choices

 - Gauge Charts: Used for immediate cognitive feedback on sales/profit "Finish Lines."

 - Donut & Bar Charts: Optimized for regional and sub-category comparisons to minimize eye strain.

 - Matrix View: Provides a granular "deep-dive" into performance metrics (Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount).

## The Data Story: Optimizing the US Retail Frontier

 1. The Hook: The $1.38M Gap

     Our story begins with a challenge. Looking at the high-level KPIs, we see a company in motion but facing a hurdle: $2.30M in actual sales against a target of $3.68M. We are at 62.5% of our goal. The dashboard was built to answer one question: Where is the missing 37.5%, and how do we capture it?

 2. The Engine: Technology’s Dominance

     When we dive into the categories, Technology emerges as the hero of the narrative. With $836,154 in sales, it is our primary revenue engine. Products like 'Phones' and 'Accessories' aren't just selling; they are maintaining healthy profit margins. This is where the company is strongest.

 3. The Villain: The "Tables" Paradox

     Every good story needs a conflict, and we found ours in the Furniture category. A startling insight revealed that while 'Tables' generate high sales volume, they are actually bleeding profit (negative margins). We are essentially paying to ship large, heavy items that aren't returning value. This "Sales without Profit" trap is a critical area for strategic intervention—either through price hikes or logistical restructuring.

 4. The Geography: The Westward Wind

     Profit isn't evenly distributed across the map. Our analysis shows a clear "West is Best" trend. The West Region accounts for 37.86% of total profits ($108.42K). While the Central region is struggling to keep pace, the West has mastered the balance between discount management and high-volume sales.

 5. The Moral: Strategy over Volume

    The data tells us that revenue is vanity, but profit is sanity. To hit our $3.68M target sustainably, the company must:

   - Double down on the Technology sector in the West.

   - Pivot the Furniture strategy to eliminate loss-making sub-categories like Tables.

   - Leverage the high-frequency "Office Supplies" category to drive customer loyalty and cross-selling.

**Summary:** By shifting focus from "Selling Everything" to "Selling Smart," we bridge the gap between our current state and our $3.68M ambition.
